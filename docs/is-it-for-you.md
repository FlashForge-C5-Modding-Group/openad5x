# Is OpenAD5X for you?

OpenAD5X replaces FlashForge's software on the AD5X with Kalico, Moonraker and a web UI.
It's in public alpha, so it suits some people more than others.

## You'll like OpenAD5X if you

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-web: **Want a full web UI**

    ---

    Fluidd or Mainsail with Moonraker, straight from the mainstream projects. You don't need the
    touchscreen at all.

-   :material-tune-variant: **Like to tune and tinker**

    ---

    Kalico, input shaper with the built-in accelerometer, load cell probing, an adaptive mesh
    switch and plain Klipper configs. See [Features](features.md).

-   :material-printer-3d: **Want the IFS in Klipper**

    ---

    The four-lane IFS runs as an AFC unit, with the stock cut, purge chute, wipes, auto-load
    and unload. See [IFS](ifs/index.md).

-   :material-shield-off: **Want FlashForge's software gone**

    ---

    FlashForge's `firmwareExe` no longer runs; Kalico, Moonraker and GrumpyScreen do its job.

</div>

## OpenAD5X may not be for you yet if you

- **Rely on the stock touchscreen.** GrumpyScreen replaces it fully, with a new UI.
- **Use FlashForge's app or cloud.** These go away with FlashForge's software.
- **Care about your warranty.** Installing mods may void it.
- **Want official support.** You can't ask FlashForge for software support if you're on custom firmware.
- **Have zero prior experience with 3D printing.** You're expected to have at least a basic understanding of Klipper.

## Not sure?

Check [Status and roadmap](status-and-roadmap.md) for what's finished. If OpenAD5X sounds right, head to
[Getting started](getting-started.md), and ask in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/RsdkqDtfww) if you have
